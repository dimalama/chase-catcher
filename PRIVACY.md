# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Chase Catcher is a browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, and Safari. This policy explains what it does
with your data. Short version: **it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.**

## What the extension accesses

- It runs only on `https://secure.chase.com`. On that site it reads the offers page in your browser to
  find offer tiles and clicks them when you press **Start**.
- It does not read, store, or transmit your login details, account numbers, balances, transactions,
  or any other personal or financial information.

## What the extension stores

Using your browser's local extension storage, on your device only:

- the number of offers activated (shown as "All-time: N offers caught");
- the date of the last run;
- whether you accepted the disclaimer, and when.

You can erase this at any time by removing the extension.

## What the extension sends

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests of its own, has no analytics, no tracking, no ads,
and no remote code. It does not sell or share data with anyone, because it has none.

## Third parties

The app stores that distribute the extension (Mozilla, Apple, Google) have their own privacy policies
covering store accounts, downloads, and crash reports. Chase's own website is governed by Chase's privacy
policy. Chase Catcher is not affiliated with JPMorgan Chase & Co.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file in the project's GitHub repository. Questions:
open an issue at <https://github.com/dimalama/chase-catcher/issues>.
